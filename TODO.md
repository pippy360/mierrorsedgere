## Movement, Parkour Physics & First-Person Animation
*(Refs: `MODLOG.md` §10.4 & §13.4, `docs/ANIMATION_SYSTEM_RE.md` §6.3, `docs/FIRST_PERSON_ANIMATION_RE.md`, `src/physics/parkour_controller.*`, `src/anim/anim_system.*`, `src/anim/fp_pose.*`)*

- **Remaining `CalcCamera` first-person camera animations:** Only `fallinglandroll` (skill roll) currently drives `camera_animation()`. Enable and verify `EyeJoint` + swizzled `CameraJoint` motion across hard landings, ladder entries, and hang-free turns (`docs/ANIMATION_SYSTEM_RE.md` §6.3). (Heave-ups are verified against retail: `MODLOG.md` §30.)
- **Ledges retail catches that the port misses:** a lip with the floor behind it up to `MaxStepHeight` higher is refused (`try_initiate_ledge_grab` wants standing or crouching room at the lip's own height; retail's `TdMove_GrabPullUp` looks for `FloorOverLedgeLocation` up to `MaxStepHeight` above it and steps up when collision returns at 1.4 s: Escape_p `(14837, 905)`, lip 11198.6, floor 25 uu higher, recordings 2026-09-20 14:56 / 09-23 01:42 / 09-25 16:06). A ledge reached falling fast is caught by retail about 46 uu from the wall and missed by the port (45 uu reach, the next frame too low; 2026-09-25 16:06 at -1255 uu/s). Replaying 2026-09-20 18:59 the port wall-climbs where retail caught the ledge.
- **Folded hang and `HangFoldedHeaveUp`:** retail hangs folded (`hangfoldedstart`) when caught with the feet on the wall and heaves up in 1.2 s (root +181.5 up within 0.48 s, `EnableCollision` and `ReleaseCamera` at 0.8 s; recordings 2026-09-20 18:59 at 82.2 s, edge_pt1 at 180.9 s). The port always plays `HangHeaveUp` / `HangFreeHeaveUp`.
- **`SmoothOffset` never engages on a static-mesh floor:** the walking eye smoothing in `ParkourController::step` requires `m_base_actor < 0`, but every static mesh floor has an actor index, so steps and stairs are smoothed only on BSP (found measuring the heave's landing, which is now smoothed on any floor).
- **Skill roll lower-body viewmodel offset (`is_lower`):** During `fallinglandroll`, Faith's legs still inherit the fixed standing lower-body offset in `evaluate_faith_1p` (54 uu back, 16° tilt), displacing them from the roll's authored pose (`src/anim/anim_system.cpp`).
- **Walk viewmodel skin clipping at 200–260 uu/s:** Large skin-colored polygons from the upper-body viewmodel clip into the screen edges at slow walk/jog speeds (`200–260 uu/s`, including right after exiting a skill roll).
- **Post-roll / diagonal walk acceleration gap (`seg08`):** Walking back-right after a skill roll caps around `350–390 uu/s` in the port whereas retail accelerates to `~610 uu/s` (`MODLOG.md` §13.4).
- **Gameplay standing eye offset (`12 uu` cutscene hand-over pop):** Retail's standing `1P` pose places the camera at `+158.7 uu` Z and `+8.6 uu` forward from feet, whereas gameplay `kEyeHeightStand` sits at `+166.0 uu` Z / `0.0 uu` forward, causing a 12 uu pop when level intros and cutscenes hand control back (`docs/LEVEL_INTROS.md` §5).
- **Post-handover turn-in-place (`StandTurn90Left` / `StandTurn90Right`):** Replicate retail's 90° turn-in-place animation and cloth/sneak step notifies when control returns in Flight, Jacknife, Ropeburn, Pirandello Kruger, and The Boat (`docs/LEVEL_INTROS.md` §5).
- **First-person skeletal controls not yet compared with retail on screen:** Both arms going with the view while she runs unarmed, the weapon's and hand's lazy springs, the hands on a sloped ledge and on a vault, the left hand on a rifle are from the executable's rules only; at retail's flat wall the port's hands sit about 1.5 uu nearer the middle of the picture. Not run: the forearm roll controls with their morph targets, and `CameraNoiseControlRoll` / `Pitch` (`docs/FIRST_PERSON_ANIMATION_RE.md` §10-11).
- **Zipline entry glide & impact animations:** Port `TdMove_IntoZipLine`'s ~0.3 s entry glide, `ziplinestart` / `ziplinehitwall` animations, and end-of-cable impact camera motion (`MODLOG.md` §10.4).

## Rendering, Lighting & Post-Processing
*(Refs: `docs/RENDERING_RE.md` §16, `MODLOG.md` §9.4, §14, §23, §24, §29 & §31, `src/renderer/*`, `src/assets/level_*`; research notes in the main checkout's ignored `build/re/notes/`)*

- **Particle emitters left out:** the PhysX type-data emitters (`ParticleModuleTypeDataMeshPhysX`: 4 in Heat's opening area), and any emitter with an attractor or a collision module (`ME_PARTICLE_DEBUG=1` lists them). Sprites are not sorted against other translucent surfaces, and the emitter tick's order is stock UE3's, not read from the executable (`docs/RENDERING_RE.md` §13).
- **Damage events, remaining differences:** damage classes are told apart as bullet, barge and blow only, so a `SeqEvent_TakeDamage` filter that names another class is never met; a punch or kick on the ground deals no damage to level actors (`TdDmgType_Melee`); the 38 `SeqEvent_Destroyed` on `GameBreakableActor`s (exploding barrels, gas cans) are not run; a pane has no shards beyond its particle effects; a door the port opens itself (`LevelScene::barge_doors`) does not run its own sequence (`docs/GAMEPLAY_SCRIPTING_RE.md` §8).
- **Bullet impacts, remaining differences:** a bot is hit as his pawn's cylinder (radius 30, 180 high, the head from 150), all of it `PM_Character_Body`, where retail traces the bodies of his physics asset (neck and hands are `PM_Character_Head`; a shield, a bag and kevlar have effects of their own), and he takes no bullet hole (retail hands `SpawnDecal` the default decal and his mesh; whether the mesh takes it is not checked); whether a bot's shot shows its impact and hole is its distance from the player alone (`TdWeapon.EffectIsRelevant` / `IsDecalRelevant` also ask whether the shooter was drawn lately); an impact sound's `MaxRadius` only decides whether it is heard, its fall-off is the one every positioned sound has here; BSP surfaces count as the default material; a bullet hole is found by a box query, not by the hit component, and does not fade (`docs/RENDERING_RE.md` §12, §13).
- **The player's dynamic shadow has a stand-in head and torso:** it is cast by the first-person body plus two ellipsoids (`player_body_stand_in`, `src/renderer/mod_shadow.hpp`). Retail casts it from the third-person body (`TdPawnMesh3p`, `bOwnerNoSeeWithShadow`), which the port does not have (`docs/RENDERING_RE.md` §10).
- **Dynamic shadows not done:** the shadows inside the first-person depth groups (the arms' on the legs), `LightShadow_ModulateBetter` while hanging from a ledge, pre-shadows.
- **Light environments, remaining differences:** the whole first-person mesh takes the arms' environment (`MyLightEnvironment1P`), where retail gives the legs the pawn's; an actor whose environment is switched off is lit by the sky colours and its one brightest light, where retail adds a pass for every light on its channels; `SkeletalMeshActor`s, dropped weapons and pickups get none (`docs/RENDERING_RE.md` §9).
- **Lens flares riding a mover:** the 89 `LensFlareSource`s on a base (police cars, trains) stay where the level placed them. A flare's coverage comes from sight lines against meshes that have collision, not from an occlusion query, so a mesh with no collision does not hide it (`docs/RENDERING_RE.md` §11).
- **Decals, remaining differences:** a decal's own `FLightMap1D` (10 receivers) is not used, nor its own depth bias; the decals computed at load (the 440 with no stored receiver) lie on static meshes and BSP only, not on movers, doors or lift cabs; `ME_DECAL_SELFCHECK=1` puts the computed clipping at 1.12 of the stored area in Jacknife, 44 of 621 decals outside 0.8..1.25 (`docs/RENDERING_RE.md` §12).
- **Material effects not driven:** taser, explosion, flashbang, laser and scope effects of the chain, and the slideshow.
- **OpenGL on Intel's Windows driver:** `me_glsl.exe --builtin-only` compiles the built-in shaders there, but `mirrorsedge_opengl.exe` and the materials were run on NVIDIA's driver only (`docs/LINUX_PORT.md`).
- **Honour `CastShadow=False` and Kismet-toggled lights/volumes:** Skip shadow-map rendering for static mesh components with `CastShadow=False` (`MODLOG.md` §9.4) and support Kismet actions that toggle lights, material scalar/vector parameters, and post-process volumes at runtime.

## Audio, Dialogue & Interactive Music
*(Refs: `docs/AUDIO_SYSTEM_RE.md`, `src/audio/audio_engine.*`)*

- **Surface-aware footsteps during level intros & cutscenes:** Trace floor physical materials (`TdPhysicalMaterialFootSteps`) beneath the animated root during cutscenes instead of defaulting to concrete (`docs/LEVEL_INTROS.md` §5).
- **Room acoustics, occlusion & `SoundGroupEffects` filter presets:** Implement UE3/DICE inside/outside room low-pass filtering, obstruction attenuation, and the 11 global `SoundGroupEffects` presets from `DefaultEngine.ini` (`docs/AUDIO_SYSTEM_RE.md` §2.3).
*Audio audit of 2026-10-10 (`MODLOG.md` §20): each item below was checked against the port's code path and against retail's cue graphs, level data, scripts or ini.*

- **Looping cues started by Kismet or by name play one wave once:** `play_layered_cue` refuses any cue with a `SoundNodeLooping`; `play_sound` / `play_sound_3d` pick one wave from the flattened list and never loop. 376 actions on 68 cues: `Elevator.Elevator_Move`, `Cops.Sirens`, `MetalGate.Moving`, the trains, `Helicopter.Helicopter` (also the hard-coded one in `main.cpp`), `Rats.feet`, `Steam_Puzzle.Big`, the radios. Needs a live cue instance: loop until Stop (with `FadeOutTime`), all mixer layers, `LoopCount`, no finite `Finished`.
- **Cue graphs are only evaluated on the mixer path:** elsewhere `SoundNodeDelay` is dropped (16 voice cues start 0.13 to 3.3 s early and cut the line before), `SoundNodeModulator` ranges become a fixed 4% wobble (`Rats.feet`, pitch 4.0, plays as a ladder hand slap), and `SoundNodeRandom` is one uniform pick over every wave in the graph. Retail's Random also never repeats an input until all are used (footsteps alternate two wave sets; a vehicle pack never sounds two horns in a row).
- **Cues are keyed by bare name without their package:** `sound_cues_` keeps the last loaded `Name` / `Group.Name`; about 52 of 2,822 Kismet sounds land on another package's cue (cutscene `Cues.Faith` stems, `Fire3P`, Flight's pillar `Big` replaced by the steam puzzle's).
- **Stopping:** `stop_cue` re-resolves the cue (a new random wave) and scans only the pool, so a Stop misses multi-wave cues and never reaches the voice source: a skipped cutscene's dialogue plays on. A checkpoint reload or level change stops nothing, then the restarted Kismet plays its sounds again on top; the ambient sources are not stopped when the next level loads.
- **Distance:** `SoundNodeAttenuation` radii are never read (they are `RawDistributionFloat` structs): every cue and emitter gets 200 / 3000 uu, where vehicle packs carry 18,000 to 27,000 and small props 1,500 or less. Positional one-shots use a fixed 2.5 m / 35 m falloff that never reaches silence, so every level-start Kismet sound is heard at once wherever it is.
- **One-shots latch the bus gain and slow-motion pitch they started with** (`start_voice`, `play_sound`): only stems, wind and emitters follow a mode change.
- **Dialogue:** one voice source, so of a cutscene's parallel per-speaker tracks only the last started is heard and any new line cuts the one playing (22 places start 2 or 3 at once: CS03, CS05, CS08, CS11, ...). `DialogueAI` chatter, news broadcasts and cop barks (136 actions) play as Faith's radio: not positional, full volume, ducking the mix. Sounds aimed at run-time targets (spawned bots, helicopters) play in the player's head.
- **Ambient emitter pool:** the four slots are dealt by distance rank every frame, so a loop restarts and a delay cue's wait is cut whenever two emitters swap rank, and only four ever sound (retail: every emitter plays from level start, attenuated). Only `*_Aud.me1` files are read: prefab-instanced emitters and those in other sublevels (AC units, fans, vents, transformers; 5,581 of 7,231) are missing, as are the 2 `AmbientSoundSimple` actors and the stereo room tone of the 332 `TdReverbVolume`s.
- **Ambient cue shapes still played wrongly:** layered cues play one layer (`WindHard` is a city bed mixed with three looping layers of gusts; `A_Prop_SecurityCamera` restarts its round when the first layer ends); a loop over a Random repeats one wave where retail picks again every round (79 emitters: `GapWind`, `Wind.Wind`, `Waves_general`, the dripping water); a delay above the loop node is drawn every round where retail waits once (16 emitters: `Horns&Boats`, `DullHammering`, `WallCreaks`); `TdSoundNodeVelocity` whoosh emitters (43 pillars and iron bars) hiss all the time where retail only sounds them as she runs past; cues with no loop node loop.
- **Footsteps and foley are not driven by the animations:** steps come from a timer in `main.cpp` (0.45 s at a walk where retail's cycle gives 0.67 to 1.0 s; about 11% too often running; always Concrete), and the sequences' sound notifies are ignored: landings are silent, a wall run gets one step for retail's five to eight, crouch-walking none, hangs / climbs / vaults lose their hand, foot, clothing and voice sounds.
- **Vocals and foley at the wrong moment:** Faith grunts (`Strain_Medium_Cue`) on every jump, where retail has no jump vocal; punches, kicks and springboards play the vault foley; every disarm press, a miss included, plays an arm break at once. Her hurt vocal (`Oral_Impact.Hard`), melee impact sounds and the look-at click never play.
- **Moves that hold a loop in retail are one-shots:** slide (the surface's `_11_*FootStepSlide`, faded in 0.1 s and out 0.5 s, shaped by speed), zip line (a synthesized tone here) and swing. Run wind: a synthesized `FX_WindGust` fires each time speed climbs back through 695 uu/s, the cue's `HighSpeedClothing` loop is missing, and the loop ignores vertical speed. Left from fixed items: breathing is still a timer, not the notifies; the fall wind is two one-shots, not the looping `Faith.Death_Fall`.
- **Weapons:** fire intervals are the port's own (Neostead +77%, Minimi +60%, Remington +46% faster than the classes' `FireInterval`), every gun plays the Beretta's `Fire1P`, there are no reverb tails, enemy and helicopter gunfire is silent. The gunshot and helicopter cues restart every frame while the simulation is not stepping (pause menu, hint card, cutscene) if their one-tick flags were set.
- **The bag-found jingle plays at every checkpoint,** script teleport, cutscene start and level change (`CheckpointChime` -> `Cues.BagFound`); retail's checkpoints are silent and the jingle belongs to bag pickups, which lack it.
- **Level audio starts at level load:** the chapter's music and emitters play under the loading movie and on top of the Prologue intro's own music.
- **macOS: three paths still play the old buffer after a stop:** Apple's OpenAL applies an `AL_BUFFER` bind made right after `alSourceStop` on a playing source only a moment later (no AL error; until then the source holds the old buffer, and `alSourcePlay` restarts it; `MODLOG.md` §22). The music stems and run wind check their bind (`AudioEngine::bind_source_buffer`); these do not: `play_vo` started over a line still playing (the old line restarts instead of the new one), a pool source stolen by `acquire_source`, and an ambient slot handed to another emitter in `update()` step 5 (it keeps the previous emitter's wave).
- **Pause menu and hint card:** the main-menu theme replaces the level's music, which restarts from the beginning on resume (retail turns the same track down to a quarter); breathing and run wind (on the hint card also footsteps and emitters) keep playing from the frozen speed; voice lines and one-shots run on, where retail pauses every non-UI sound.
- **Mix presets:** falling ducks music to 12% where the ini only mutes dialogue; during death everything but music stays audible; dialogue is on no bus. `SeqAct_TdFadeEffect.bFadeSound2` (the sound fading with the screen, at the end of nearly every chapter) is ignored.
- **Music:** four tracks loop at once and are mixed by Faith's run speed; retail plays one track chosen by the level's Kismet (`*_Mus` sublevels, `SeqAct_CrossFadeMusicTracks`), or none. The Training Area plays the Prologue's music where retail has none; reaction time fades the combat track up where retail only turns the playing music down to 70%; time trials bind story stems instead of one track from countdown to finish; the front end drops `PlayMusic`, so the 20 unlockable tracks never play.
- **Localized elevator announcements (`SeqCond_TdCaseLanguage`):** Select the active locale link on `SeqCond_TdCaseLanguage` instead of firing all output pins simultaneously (`src/game/level_script.cpp`).
