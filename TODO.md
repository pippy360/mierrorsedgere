## Audio, Dialogue & Interactive Music
*(Refs: `docs/AUDIO_SYSTEM_RE.md`, `src/audio/audio_engine.*`)*

- **Surface-aware footsteps during level intros & cutscenes:** Trace floor physical materials (`TdPhysicalMaterialFootSteps`) beneath the animated root during cutscenes instead of defaulting to concrete (`docs/LEVEL_INTROS.md` §5).
- **Room acoustics, occlusion & `SoundGroupEffects` filter presets:** Implement UE3/DICE inside/outside room low-pass filtering, obstruction attenuation, and the 11 global `SoundGroupEffects` presets from `DefaultEngine.ini` (`docs/AUDIO_SYSTEM_RE.md` §2.3).
*Audio audit of 2026-10-10 (`MODLOG.md` §20): each item below was checked against the port's code path and against retail's cue graphs, level data, scripts or ini.*

- **Looping cues started by Kismet or by name play one wave once:** `play_layered_cue` refuses any cue with a `SoundNodeLooping`; `play_sound` / `play_sound_3d` pick one wave from the flattened list and never loop. 376 actions on 68 cues: `Elevator.Elevator_Move`, `Cops.Sirens`, `MetalGate.Moving`, the trains, `Helicopter.Helicopter` (also the hard-coded one in `main.cpp`), `Rats.feet`, `Steam_Puzzle.Big`, the radios. Needs a live cue instance: loop until Stop (with `FadeOutTime`), all mixer layers, `LoopCount`, no finite `Finished`.
- **Cue graphs are only evaluated on the mixer path:** elsewhere `SoundNodeDelay` is dropped (16 voice cues start 0.13 to 3.3 s early and cut the line before), `SoundNodeModulator` ranges become a fixed 4% wobble (`Rats.feet`, pitch 4.0, plays as a ladder hand slap), and `SoundNodeRandom` is one uniform pick over every wave in the graph. Retail's Random also never repeats an input until all are used (footsteps alternate two wave sets; a vehicle pack never sounds two horns in a row).
- **Cues are keyed by bare name without their package:** `sound_cues_` keeps the last loaded `Name` / `Group.Name`; about 52 of 2,822 Kismet sounds land on another package's cue (cutscene `Cues.Faith` stems, `Fire3P`, Flight's pillar `Big` replaced by the steam puzzle's).
- **Stopping:** `stop_cue` re-resolves the cue (a new random wave) and scans only the pool, so a Stop misses multi-wave cues and never reaches the voice source: a skipped cutscene's dialogue plays on. A checkpoint reload or level change stops nothing, then the restarted Kismet plays its sounds again on top; the ambient sources are not stopped when the next level loads.
- **Distance, what is left:** a sound at a place now falls off by its cue's own attenuation nodes (`docs/AUDIO_SYSTEM_RE.md` §3.2, `MODLOG.md` §37). Not done:
  - *The low pass with distance* (`bAttenuateWithLowPassFilter`, on for 1,689 of the 1,828 nodes): `LPFMinRadius` / `LPFMaxRadius` are read and kept (`SoundAttenuation`), nothing applies them. Whether retail's PC device does anything with `CurrentHighFrequencyGain` has not been traced.
  - *Occlusion* (`bCheckSoundOcclusion`: `ClientHearSound` halves the volume of a sound `HearSound`'s line check found blocked; the component's `OcclusionVolumeDuckLevel` 0.3), and the sound groups' `MaxUpdateDistance` (2000 / 3000 for the indoor / outdoor ambient props), whose use in the executable was not found.
  - *The listener point:* the pawn's location plus her eye height (`main.cpp`). Which point retail's listener is was not traced; `HearSound` measures to the view target's `Location`.
  - *`TdSoundNodeRelativePosition`* (126 cues) moves the source before the attenuation measures; the port passes it through.
  - *Which test a sound must pass to start:* `HearSound`'s (any length of cue) is applied to bullet impacts only; every other sound at a place gets `CreateComponent`'s (cues of at most a second). Which of the two retail's `SeqAct_PlaySound`, `SeqAct_TdPlaySound` and `AnimNotify_Sound` go through was not traced. A lead: Engine.u's `PlayerController.Kismet_ClientPlaySound` makes its component with `SourceActor.CreateAudioComponent(ASound, false, true)`, no location, so `CreateComponent`'s test would never refuse it; that `SeqAct_PlaySound` is what calls it was not read. Not read either: that `Actor.PlaySound` is what calls `HearSound`, and that the length `CreateComponent`'s test compares with a second is the cue's duration (`docs/AUDIO_SYSTEM_RE.md` §3.2).
  - *Small things:* a `TdSoundNodeAttenuation` takes its first distance when the cue starts, not after the delays above it; an emitter's radii are drawn once, where stock UE3's `SoundNodeLooping` draws the ones below it again every round (not read in the executable); a wave asked for at a place by its own name, with no cue, gets the class defaults (400 / 5000).
  - *By ear:* the gains were checked as numbers, without an audio device. Nobody has listened to it, and nothing was held against a retail recording.
- **One-shots latch the bus gain and slow-motion pitch they started with** (`start_voice`, `play_sound`): only stems, wind and emitters follow a mode change.
- **Dialogue:** one voice source, so of a cutscene's parallel per-speaker tracks only the last started is heard and any new line cuts the one playing (22 places start 2 or 3 at once: CS03, CS05, CS08, CS11, ...). `DialogueAI` chatter, news broadcasts and cop barks (136 actions) play as Faith's radio: not positional, full volume, ducking the mix. Sounds aimed at run-time targets (spawned bots, helicopters) play in the player's head.
- **Ambient emitter pool:** the four slots are dealt by distance rank every frame, so a loop restarts and a delay cue's wait is cut whenever two emitters swap rank, and only four ever sound (retail: every emitter plays from level start, attenuated). The four are the nearest that are heard at all, not the loudest: an emitter a few units inside its `MaxRadius`, or in the last decibels of a NaturalSound curve, takes a source ahead of a louder one farther off (90 of 17,894 sources at a distance gain of at most 0.002 over a walk of the eleven maps' emitters, `MODLOG.md` §38; stock UE3 hands its channels to the loudest wave instances, which was not read in this executable). Only `*_Aud.me1` files are read: prefab-instanced emitters and those in other sublevels (AC units, fans, vents, transformers; 5,581 of 7,231) are missing, as is the stereo room tone of the 332 `TdReverbVolume`s. An emitter's level is its component's `VolumeMultiplier` times a fixed 0.55: its cue's own `VolumeMultiplier` (0.3 to 2 across the emitters' cues, 0.75 on half of them) is not applied.
- **Ambient cue shapes still played wrongly:** layered cues play one layer (`WindHard` is a city bed mixed with three looping layers of gusts; `A_Prop_SecurityCamera` restarts its round when the first layer ends), and where the layers have attenuation nodes of their own (767 of the 1,650 emitters) always the one that carries farthest, the first in the graph when two carry equally far: an indoor vent, pipe or AC unit is its far layer alone at every distance (`A_Prop_AirVent_Indoor`: the hum, Logarithmic 150 / 3000, never the hiss, Logarithmic 2 / 1500, that retail mixes in close by); a loop over a Random repeats one wave where retail picks again every round (79 emitters: `GapWind`, `Wind.Wind`, `Waves_general`, the dripping water); a delay above the loop node is drawn every round where retail waits once (16 emitters: `Horns&Boats`, `DullHammering`, `WallCreaks`); `TdSoundNodeVelocity` whoosh emitters (43 pillars and iron bars) hiss all the time where retail only sounds them as she runs past; cues with no loop node loop.
- **Footsteps and foley are not driven by the animations:** steps come from a timer in `main.cpp` (0.45 s at a walk where retail's cycle gives 0.67 to 1.0 s; about 11% too often running; always Concrete), and the sequences' sound notifies are ignored: landings are silent, a wall run gets one step for retail's five to eight, crouch-walking none, hangs / climbs / vaults lose their hand, foot, clothing and voice sounds.
