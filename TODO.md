The sources' low-pass filters are there since `MODLOG.md` §44; a sound mode's `LowPassAdjuster` and an occluded sound's `AdjustLowPassFiltering` (to 0.3, with its volume) are two more factors of the same high-frequency gain (`docs/AUDIO_SYSTEM_RE.md` §3.2).
*Audio audit of 2026-10-10 (`MODLOG.md` §20): each item below was checked against the port's code path and against retail's cue graphs, level data, scripts or ini.*

- **Looping cues started by Kismet or by name play one wave once:** `play_layered_cue` refuses any cue with a `SoundNodeLooping`; `play_sound` / `play_sound_3d` pick one wave from the flattened list and never loop. 376 actions on 68 cues: `Elevator.Elevator_Move`, `Cops.Sirens`, `MetalGate.Moving`, the trains, `Helicopter.Helicopter` (also the hard-coded one in `main.cpp`), `Rats.feet`, `Steam_Puzzle.Big`, the radios. Needs a live cue instance: loop until Stop (with `FadeOutTime`), all mixer layers, `LoopCount`, no finite `Finished`.
- **Cue graphs are only evaluated on the mixer path:** elsewhere `SoundNodeDelay` is dropped (16 voice cues start 0.13 to 3.3 s early and cut the line before), `SoundNodeModulator` ranges become a fixed 4% wobble (`Rats.feet`, pitch 4.0, plays as a ladder hand slap), and `SoundNodeRandom` is one uniform pick over every wave in the graph. Retail's Random also never repeats an input until all are used (footsteps alternate two wave sets; a vehicle pack never sounds two horns in a row).
- **Cues are keyed by bare name without their package:** `sound_cues_` keeps the last loaded `Name` / `Group.Name`; about 52 of 2,822 Kismet sounds land on another package's cue (cutscene `Cues.Faith` stems, `Fire3P`, Flight's pillar `Big` replaced by the steam puzzle's).
- **Stopping:** `stop_cue` re-resolves the cue (a new random wave) and scans only the pool, so a Stop misses multi-wave cues and never reaches the voice source: a skipped cutscene's dialogue plays on. A checkpoint reload or level change stops nothing of the one-shots, then the restarted Kismet plays its sounds again on top. (The emitters' voices are stopped by the next level's load since `MODLOG.md` §44: checked with no device only.)
- **Distance, what is left:** a sound at a place now falls off by its cue's own attenuation nodes (`docs/AUDIO_SYSTEM_RE.md` §3.2, `MODLOG.md` §37). Not done:
  - *The low pass with distance, what is left* (`docs/AUDIO_SYSTEM_RE.md` §3.2, `MODLOG.md` §44): it is worked out for every sound at a place and set on its source's EFX low-pass filter as retail's device sets it. Not known: whether the OpenAL retail ships (`wrap_oal.dll` behind the router, device `Generic Hardware`) grants EFX, an effect slot, a send and the filters on a retail machine, so whether retail's players hear it at all (a run of the game, or of that library, with an OpenAL shim that logs `alFilterf` would say). macOS: Apple's OpenAL is not expected to have EFX, so the value is worked out and nothing is applied there (not run).   - *`MaxUpdateDistance`* (2000 / 3000 for the indoor / outdoor ambient props) is applied to the level emitters only; a one-shot of those sound groups is not cut by it.
  - *Doppler:* retail gives every source its wave's velocity and never sets a Doppler factor or a speed of sound (the library's defaults apply; positions and velocities are scaled by 0.0127, the listener's velocity is 0). The port sets no velocity. Whether anything in retail is heard to shift was not looked at.
  - *The listener point:* the pawn's location plus her eye height (`main.cpp`). Which point retail's listener is was not traced; `HearSound` measures to the view target's `Location`.
  - *`TdSoundNodeRelativePosition`* (126 cues) moves the source before the attenuation measures; the port passes it through.
  - *Which test a sound must pass to start:* `HearSound`'s (any length of cue) is applied to bullet impacts only; every other sound at a place gets `CreateComponent`'s (cues of at most a second). Which of the two retail's `SeqAct_PlaySound`, `SeqAct_TdPlaySound` and `AnimNotify_Sound` go through was not traced. A lead: Engine.u's `PlayerController.Kismet_ClientPlaySound` makes its component with `SourceActor.CreateAudioComponent(ASound, false, true)`, no location, so `CreateComponent`'s test would never refuse it; that `SeqAct_PlaySound` is what calls it was not read. Not read either: that `Actor.PlaySound` is what calls `HearSound`, and that the length `CreateComponent`'s test compares with a second is the cue's duration (`docs/AUDIO_SYSTEM_RE.md` §3.2).
  - *Small things:* a `TdSoundNodeAttenuation` takes its first distance when the cue starts, not after the delays above it; a wave asked for at a place by its own name, with no cue, gets the class defaults (400 / 5000, low pass 1500 / 5000); an attenuation node under an emitter's looping node draws its radii again every round since `MODLOG.md` §44, which no test has run (none of the 94 emitter cues has one there).
  - *By ear:* the gains were checked as numbers, without an audio device. Nobody has listened to it, and nothing was held against a retail recording.
- **One-shots latch the bus gain and slow-motion pitch they started with** (`start_voice`, `play_sound`): only stems, wind and emitters follow a mode change.
- **Dialogue:** one voice source, so of a cutscene's parallel per-speaker tracks only the last started is heard and any new line cuts the one playing (22 places start 2 or 3 at once: CS03, CS05, CS08, CS11, ...). `DialogueAI` chatter, news broadcasts and cop barks (136 actions) play as Faith's radio: not positional, full volume, ducking the mix. Sounds aimed at run-time targets (spawned bots, helicopters) play in the player's head.
- **Level emitters, what is left** (every wave of an emitter's cue is a voice now, parsed, ranked and given a source as retail's device does it: `docs/AUDIO_SYSTEM_RE.md` §3.3, `MODLOG.md` §44):
  - *One pool for everything.* Retail ranks every wave, of emitters, one-shots, music and dialogue alike, for its 32 sources. The port's one-shots, voice line, run wind and music are not ranked: they have sources of their own, and the emitters' waves share what those leave of 32. A one-shot at volume 0 keeps its source (retail gives it none, and a one-shot that is ranked out is finished on the spot); none is stopped at 2.5 times its cue's length. At 1,443 of 6,600 sample places more than 32 emitter waves ask for a source (84 at the most, on The Boat), so the quietest drop out and start over as the count of other sounds changes: as in retail, and not listened to.
  - *Only the `*_Aud.me1` sublevels are read:* prefab-instanced emitters and those in other sublevels (AC units, fans, vents, transformers; 5,581 of 7,231) are missing, as is the stereo room tone of the 332 `TdReverbVolume`s.
  - *Levels:* an emitter's level is its component's `VolumeMultiplier` times a fixed 0.55. Its cue's `VolumeMultiplier` (0.3 to 2 across the emitters' cues, 0.75 on half of them), its sound group's `Volume` (1.2 and 1.3 for the indoor and outdoor props) and its wave's (0.75 on every one) are not applied; nor are they in the 0.0001 below which retail gives a wave no source.
  - *Nodes passed through:* `TdSoundNodeVelocity` whoosh emitters (43 pillars and iron bars) hiss all the time where retail only sounds them as she runs past; `SoundNodeOscillator` (the transformers, 13 emitters) does nothing; a mix group node applies the ini's Volume and Pitch only (what the device's mix group object adds while the game runs, ducking and slow motion, was not read, nor whether the stale `MixGroupIDs` a cooked node carries count).
  - *Not read or not run:* that a random node and a modulator draw again when a loop above them asks is stock Unreal Engine 3 (their functions were not read); `USoundNodeAmbient`'s looping of its wave likewise (the two `AmbientSoundSimple` sirens loop as before); a cue with no looping node now plays once and its emitter stops, which no test has run (no emitter's cue is one); `A_Prop_SecurityCamera`'s and `WallWater_Discreet_StormDrain`'s rounds (a looping node over a mixer) were not looked at singly; a round that draws `BirdsChirp`'s empty input reaches no wave and the emitter stops for good, as it did before (retail's component stops when a parse finds nothing playing or waiting; that its random node parses nothing for an empty input was not read).
- **Footsteps and foley are not driven by the animations:** steps come from a timer in `main.cpp` (0.45 s at a walk where retail's cycle gives 0.67 to 1.0 s; about 11% too often running; always Concrete), and the sequences' sound notifies are ignored: landings are silent, a wall run gets one step for retail's five to eight, crouch-walking none, hangs / climbs / vaults lose their hand, foot, clothing and voice sounds.
- **Vocals and foley at the wrong moment:** Faith grunts (`Strain_Medium_Cue`) on every jump, where retail has no jump vocal; punches, kicks and springboards play the vault foley; every disarm press, a miss included, plays an arm break at once. Her hurt vocal (`Oral_Impact.Hard`), melee impact sounds and the look-at click never play.
- **Moves that hold a loop in retail are one-shots:** slide (the surface's `_11_*FootStepSlide`, faded in 0.1 s and out 0.5 s, shaped by speed), zip line (a synthesized tone here) and swing. Run wind: a synthesized `FX_WindGust` fires each time speed climbs back through 695 uu/s, the cue's `HighSpeedClothing` loop is missing, and the loop ignores vertical speed. Left from fixed items: breathing is still a timer, not the notifies; the fall wind is two one-shots, not the looping `Faith.Death_Fall`.
- **Weapons:** fire intervals are the port's own (Neostead +77%, Minimi +60%, Remington +46% faster than the classes' `FireInterval`), every gun plays the Beretta's `Fire1P`, there are no reverb tails, enemy and helicopter gunfire is silent. (The gunshot is no longer asked for again on every frame the simulation does not step, `MODLOG.md` §44: by reading, the game's loop was not run.)
- **The bag-found jingle plays at every checkpoint,** script teleport, cutscene start and level change (`CheckpointChime` -> `Cues.BagFound`); retail's checkpoints are silent and the jingle belongs to bag pickups, which lack it.
- **Level audio starts at level load:** the chapter's music and emitters play under the loading movie and on top of the Prologue intro's own music.
- **macOS: two paths still play the old buffer after a stop:** Apple's OpenAL applies an `AL_BUFFER` bind made right after `alSourceStop` on a playing source only a moment later (no AL error; until then the source holds the old buffer, and `alSourcePlay` restarts it; `MODLOG.md` §22). The music stems and run wind check their bind (`AudioEngine::bind_source_buffer`); these do not: `play_vo` started over a line still playing (the old line restarts instead of the new one), and a pool source stolen by `acquire_slot`. An emitter's wave that gets a source checks its bind and tries again at the next update since `MODLOG.md` §44, which has not been run on macOS.
- **Pause, what is left** (a paused game's sounds are paused and its music turned down as retail's are: `docs/AUDIO_SYSTEM_RE.md` §3.3, `MODLOG.md` §44): nobody has heard it, and the game's loop was not run (the engine's side is in the oracle and, on a silent device, in `--audio-device-check`; the loop's call is by reading). A tutorial message whose Kismet action has `bPauseGame` does not pause the port's game at all (`level_script.cpp` reads the flag and nothing uses it). Which cooked UI scenes carry `bPauseGameWhileActive` was not dumped: the hint card is taken for a pause by its "game paused" label. All the music is taken for the level's (`InGameMusic`): a cutscene's (`CutSceneMusic`, not a UI sound group) would be paused in retail. Retail's device also sees a game that is not ticking for the frames of a blocking level load and of a pending map change (every non-UI source paused and played on); the port does not. Whether anything native sets the sound mode 4, "Pause", was not looked for (no script does). The port's overlay menu (Tab) counts as a pause when opened from play and as the front end when the game starts in it with no front end loaded: its own rule. The quarter is put on whatever music plays, so a time trial's music (not of the group `InGameMusic` in retail, which leaves it alone) is turned down too.
- **Mix presets:** falling ducks music to 12% where the ini only mutes dialogue; during death everything but music stays audible; dialogue is on no bus. `SeqAct_TdFadeEffect.bFadeSound2` (the sound fading with the screen, at the end of nearly every chapter) is ignored.
- **Music:** four tracks loop at once and are mixed by Faith's run speed; retail plays one track chosen by the level's Kismet (`*_Mus` sublevels, `SeqAct_CrossFadeMusicTracks`), or none. The Training Area plays the Prologue's music where retail has none; reaction time fades the combat track up where retail only turns the playing music down to 70%; time trials bind story stems instead of one track from countdown to finish; the front end drops `PlayMusic`, so the 20 unlockable tracks never play.
- **Localized elevator announcements (`SeqCond_TdCaseLanguage`):** Select the active locale link on `SeqCond_TdCaseLanguage` instead of firing all output pins simultaneously (`src/game/level_script.cpp`).

## Menus, Time Trials & Platform Backends
- **The Vulkan executable does not link with MSYS2 on Windows:** `mirrorsedge_vulkan.exe` fails with undefined `spvtools::` symbols from `libshaderc_combined.a`, which MSYS2 builds without SPIRV-Tools inside (they are `libSPIRV-Tools-opt.a` and `libSPIRV-Tools.a`, to be linked after it). Building it also wants the `mingw-w64-ucrt-x86_64-vulkan-headers` package. Configure with `-DME_VULKAN=OFF` there until the CMake link line names them.
*(Refs: `docs/MAIN_MENU_SYSTEM_RE.md`, `docs/SUB_MENUS_RE.md`, `docs/WINDOWS_PORT.md`, `docs/LINUX_PORT.md`)*

- **Time Trial (`TT_*`) & Speed Run mode:** Implement race start/finish triggers (`SeqEvt_TTRace*`), checkpoint split timers, star rating targets from `TdTimeTrial` data, and ghost recording/playback.
- **Native GPU rendering for front-end menu background (`D3D11Renderer`):** Replace the slow (~21 fps) CPU reference background path on Windows front-end menus with hardware-accelerated rendering (`MODLOG.md` §11.3).
- **Linux (`gl_renderer`) native hardware testing & parallel shader upload:** Validate `me_glsl` / `mirrorsedge_linux` on native Linux Mesa/NVIDIA/AMD drivers, move material program linking off the main thread, and exercise `SDL_VIDEODRIVER=offscreen` (`docs/LINUX_PORT.md`).
- **Android beyond the first look:** the port (`docs/ANDROID_PORT.md`) was run on the emulator (OpenGL ES 3.0 translator) and started on a Pixel 9 (Mali-G715, ES 3.2: every shader compiles, the training area plays). Still to do on devices: a level played through, the touch layout tuned with fingers, an Adreno phone, the frame rate measured (`ME_RENDER_PROF` needs an environment variable; add a `me_args.txt` switch), background / foreground with a lost GL context, a Bluetooth gamepad.
- **Memory for phones:** chapter 0 keeps ~4 GB resident on macOS, 2.1–2.4 GB on the emulator and 5.4 GB PSS (3.5 GB graphics) on a Pixel 9 (measured 2026-10-11, `MODLOG.md` §41). The buckets: `load_character_assets()` decodes all 81 character / weapon DXT1 textures (mostly 2048²) to RGBA8 and builds mips at start (~0.8 GB GPU + driver copies; uploading them as BC1 cut the macOS peak 4.9 → 3.5 GB in an experiment); every stock audio bank is decoded to PCM at start (560 MB, 280 MB of it duplicates: `AudioEngine::store_clip` stores a copy under `name` and again under `full_path`); the CPU copies of `SceneMaterialLibrary::textures` (156 MB), light maps (58 MB) and `MeshBuffer::vertices` (169 MB) stay after upload. On Android the CPU DXT decode adds the uncompressed level textures (no S3TC); a transcode to ETC2 / ASTC or `ME_TEX_DROP_MIPS` is the way down.
- **Android Bink movies:** no FFmpeg on Android (`ME_NO_FFMPEG`): the chapter cutscenes are skipped. Cross-compile FFmpeg's Bink decoder with the NDK (`--enable-decoder=bink,binkaudio_dct,binkaudio_rdft --enable-demuxer=bink`) and link it in place of the stub.
- **Android shadow map at 2048:** ES drivers that apply `GL_MAX_3D_TEXTURE_SIZE` to array textures get a 2048 shadow array; the far cascade's shader offsets are still those of a 12 uu texel and only four modulated shadows fit the atlas (`src/renderer/mod_shadow.hpp`).
- **Metal windowed screenshots are blank:** in a window `MetalRenderer::render_frame` draws into the drawable, but `save_screenshot_png` (the `P` key, `--exit-screenshot`) reads `offscreen_color_tex`, which is only the target when headless; the PNG comes out white. Found while adding `--exit-screenshot`; the OpenGL and Direct3D backends are fine.
